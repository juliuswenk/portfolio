# Matrix Lamp

## At a glance

A personal gift became an exploration of ambient light, electronics, and visible construction. Five animations run across a 16 × 16 LED matrix; one knob selects the animation, colour, and brightness. Acrylic layers and a metal rear plate are held apart by threaded rods, keeping the parts accessible for repair and modification. I planned and fabricated the structure, soldered the electronics, and created the animations, with AI assistance for electronics layout and firmware. Completed in two weeks in October 2026, the lamp worked well in use, although pressing the knob can shift the object.

## Short summary

*Matrix Lamp* is an ambient light made as a personal gift. Five animations play across a 16 × 16 LED matrix, while an exposed, layered construction reveals how the object is assembled.

## Context & timeframe

A private, self-directed project built over two weeks in October 2026.

## Intention & design

The project was an opportunity to develop my electronics and fabrication skills while creating calming ambient light. I wanted the lamp to be easy to repair and modify, with its parts and their relationships visible.

## My role & assistance

I developed the build plan, fabricated the plates, assembled the structure, soldered the components, and created the animations. AI tools assisted with the electronics layout and firmware coding.

## Process & key decisions

I initially planned a bent aluminium enclosure, then switched to separate layers held together with threaded rods and nuts. This simplified fabrication, made the components accessible, and turned the construction itself into part of the lamp’s appearance.

Learning to solder and assemble the electronics was the most experimental part of the build.

## Construction & interaction

Two laser-cut acrylic sheets and a metal rear plate, cut with a mechanical saw, form the layered structure.

A single knob controls animation selection, colour, and brightness. Pressing it cycles between these three settings; turning it adjusts the selected setting.

## Finished object & use

The lamp was completed on time and given to its recipient. It worked well in use, providing the intended ambient lighting.

## Learning & reflection

The build gave me practical experience in soldering, fabrication, and assembly. It also revealed an interaction problem: pressing the knob can shift the lamp because of its placement. For another version, I would choose a different control arrangement.

## Skills & network connections

- **Physical Computing:** Electronics assembly and soldering.
- **Digital Fabrication:** Laser cutting and component fabrication.
- **Object Design:** Accessible, layered construction.
- **Interaction Design:** Animation, colour, and brightness control.
