# Matrix Lamp

Edited portfolio copy. Original project descriptions remain alongside this file.

## At a glance

Matrix Lamp is a personal gift and an exploration of electronics and fabrication. I designed and built a layered lamp around a 16 × 16 LED matrix, creating five animations for calming ambient light with AI assistance on the electronics layout and firmware. Completed over two weeks in October 2026, it worked well in use, though pressing its control knob can shift the object.

## Context

Personal gift · October 2026 · Two weeks

## Intention & design

I wanted calming ambient light in an object whose construction remains visible. Making its parts accessible would also allow the lamp to be repaired and modified.

## My role & assistance

I developed the build plan, fabricated the plates, assembled the structure, soldered the components, and created the animations. AI tools assisted with the electronics layout and firmware coding.

## Outcome & learning

The lamp was completed on time and given to its recipient. It worked well as ambient lighting, while giving me practical experience in soldering, fabrication, and assembly.

Pressing the knob can shift the lamp because of its placement. For another version, I would choose a different control arrangement.

## Key design decisions

I initially planned a bent aluminium enclosure, then switched to separate layers held together with threaded rods and nuts. This simplified fabrication, made the components accessible, and turned the construction itself into part of the lamp’s appearance.

Learning to solder and assemble the electronics was the most experimental part of the build.

## Construction & interaction

Two laser-cut acrylic sheets and a metal rear plate, cut with a mechanical saw, form the layered structure.

A single knob controls animation selection, colour, and brightness. Pressing it cycles between these three settings; turning it adjusts the selected setting.
