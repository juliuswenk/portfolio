Hi! I’m Julius

My background is in communication strategy. In design, I still start by asking what an experience communicates and what it invites people to do.

My projects use technological systems to explore the social, political, and economic structures around them. I’m interested in how their design shapes who gets to act and who depends on whom, especially when those choices remain hidden.

I work through these questions by building things people can try. I also enjoy making itself: working with materials and seeing how an idea changes when it becomes an object or an interaction.
