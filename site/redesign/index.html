Hi! I’m Julius

My background in communication strategy shapes how I approach design: what does an experience communicate, how does it frame a subject, and what does it invite people to do?

I explore these questions through interactive installations, digital experiences, and physical objects. Technological systems often become stand-ins for the social, political, and economic structures surrounding us. I’m interested in the choices and interests built into them—who gets to act, who depends on whom, and what remains hidden.

Making lets me turn these questions into something people can encounter and experiment with. It’s also something I enjoy in its own right: working with materials, shaping behaviour, and discovering how an idea changes when it becomes a working object or experience.
